🚀 Case Studies

Real businesses. Real challenges. Real results. Here’s how I’ve helped brands grow and stand out.

Client: Soundhous

Challenge

  • Soundhous, the official distributor of Sonos speakers in Nigeria, was a brand-new company in Victoria Island, Lagos.
  • Their brand presence in Nigeria was non-existent — nobody knew them.
  • Their e-commerce platform was clunky, not user-friendly, and didn’t reflect the luxury positioning of Sonos.
  • When customers searched “Sonos speakers in Nigeria”, Soundhous didn’t appear at all.

Solution

  • Redesigned and managed the e-commerce platform to feel premium, fast, and seamless for luxury clients.
  • Implemented full e-commerce management to ensure efficiency and high conversion rates.
  • Set up email marketing automation for targeting, upselling, and cross-selling customers.
  • Carried out deep SEO research and optimization so Soundhous could rank for high-value keywords like “Sonos speakers in Nigeria” and “buy Sonos speakers in Nigeria.”

Results

  • Top 3 ranking on Google for Sonos-related searches in Nigeria — without paid ads.
  • Over $20,000 in sales generated in a single month through the new e-commerce platform.
  • Over $6,000 in revenue directly from organic SEO efforts in just one month, with $0 spent on ads.
  • Soundhous quickly established itself as the luxury destination for premium audio in Lagos.

🔗 Visit Website: www.soundhous.com

Client: Platform Capital

Challenge

  • Platform Capital — one of Nigeria’s top investment firms — needed a complete brand transformation.
  • Their existing digital presence did not reflect their global impact and leadership in empowering businesses across emerging markets.
  • They required consistency across all touchpoints — from internal communications to investor-facing materials.

Solution

  • Led a full 360° rebranding — including a refreshed identity system, corporate brochures, and advisory document designs.
  • Developed a new world-class website that aligned with their global ambitions and positioned them as a thought leader: www.theplatformcapital.com
  • Redesigned the structure of newsletters and investor updates for clarity, professionalism, and impact.
  • Created a communication framework for both internal and external audiences, ensuring cohesion across all messaging.
  • Managed social media presence and content creation to showcase investments, thought leadership, and global partnerships.

Results

  • Transformed Platform Capital’s image into a globally recognizable investment powerhouse.
  • Strengthened investor confidence and credibility with international stakeholders.
  • Boosted digital engagement and visibility across social platforms and online channels.
  • Delivered a unified brand experience that matched their ambition of reshaping the future of businesses across Africa and beyond.

🔗 Visit Website: www.theplatformcapital.com

Client: Spectra AVL

Challenge

  • Spectra AVL had zero brand presence — no logo, no website, no social media, no Google profile, nothing.
  • Their expertise in AV integration wasn’t visible to clients or partners, making them almost non-existent online.

Solution

  • Created a complete 360° brand identity — logo, visual system, brand guidelines, and corporate materials.
  • Designed and launched a sleek, modern website optimized for credibility and international positioning.
  • Set up their Google Business account and ensured search visibility.
  • Crafted case-driven messaging and sales assets to attract global partners.

Results

  • From complete non-existence to a recognizable, trusted AV integrator brand in less than 6 months.
  • Strengthened credibility with global manufacturers and partners.
  • Built long-term brand assets that positioned Spectra AVL as a premium AV leader in West Africa.
